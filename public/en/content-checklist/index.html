Preparing your project

The content checklist: ten answers is enough

About 20 minutes · no account to create

The $799 CAD site asks you to supply the logo, photos and approved copy. That is not a trick: it is what keeps the price low and the scope clear. Here is exactly what I need. Answer in your own words — I handle the structure, the layout and both languages.

You do not need all of it today. Send what you have and I will tell you what is missing.

01

Your exact business name

As it must appear everywhere: site, invoices, Google. If you have a legal name and a trading name, tell me which one to use.

02

Your logo

The original file if you have it (SVG, PDF or PNG). If all you have is a photo of your truck, a screenshot of your Facebook page works too.

03

Your contact details

Email, phone number, and a link to your Google listing or social pages if you have them. This is what the visitor will use to reach you.

04

Your services, in plain words

What you do, written the way you would explain it to a customer on the phone. One sentence per service is enough. No need to write for a search engine.

05

Your service area and towns

The towns or neighbourhoods you actually drive to. Useful for a plumber or landscaper; unnecessary if you do not have an area.

06

Three to six photos

Real photos of your work. The ones your customers already see on your phone will do. A photo of your team or your workshop helps a lot.

07

Which language you are starting from

French, English, or both. If you only have the text in one language, tell me and I will explain your options before we start.

08

The one action a visitor should take

Fill in a form, call you, email you, or request a quote. One main action only.

09

Three questions your customers always ask

For example: do you give free estimates, how long do you take, do you take small jobs. These answers cut half of your unnecessary calls.

10

Anything your trade requires you to display

Licence number, RBQ number, insurance, professional order. If the law requires it to be shown, it belongs on the page.

Deadline

You have ten days after the slot is reserved to send me your content. Past that, the project moves to the following month and your deposit is carried over to that new date at no charge. Nobody loses their slot.

How to send it

Easiest: fill in the form on the homepage and describe your business in a few lines. I reply within two business days with a fit decision and the next available slot. If you would rather write directly: amitt.bhardwj@gmail.com.
