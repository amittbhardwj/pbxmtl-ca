One new project per month · 50% deposit on confirmation

Slots are deliberately limited to one new project per month. This page explains exactly how a slot is booked, so nothing is ambiguous before we start.

01

You send your inquiry

A few lines about your business, your trade and the month that would suit you.

02

I confirm the fit

A reply within two business days with a clear decision and the next available month.

03

The month is reserved by the deposit

The 50% deposit ($399.50 CAD) is payable by Interac e-transfer or invoice. Nothing is charged before step 2, and no card is requested on this site.

04

The balance before launch

The remaining 50% is due after the revision round and before the site goes live.

What stays on your side

  • the logo, photos and final approved copy, in French and English;
  • the domain name and hosting, which remain in your name;
  • one point of contact for decisions, so the 14–21 day schedule holds.
