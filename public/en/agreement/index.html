Engagement terms · plain summary

How an engagement works

Plain-language summary · the signed proposal reaches you before work starts

This is a plain-language summary, not the contract. Before any work starts I send you a written proposal to read and sign. It states the scope, the price, the timeline and what happens when something changes.

Scope

  • every engagement has a written scope: an audit, a build or monthly care;
  • a change of scope gets a new written estimate before any extra work starts;
  • anything a customer sees is approved by a person on your side, unless you decide otherwise in writing.

What is not included

  • systems that make financial, medical, legal or hiring decisions without a person;
  • emergency or unlimited support;
  • third-party costs such as cloud hosting and AI model usage, which are billed to your own accounts.

Price and payment

  • a fixed price in CAD, plus applicable taxes;
  • a 50% deposit starts the work and the balance is due on delivery;
  • payable by Interac e-transfer or invoice;
  • monthly care is billed month to month and can be cancelled at any time.

Deposit refunds

  • if the work has not started: the deposit is refunded in full on request, less any transfer fees actually incurred;
  • once work has started: the deposit is not refundable, but it covers work already done, which is handed to you;
  • if I cannot deliver: full refund, no argument.

Delays

If the project waits more than ten (10) days for access, data or answers from your side, I move the remaining work to the next open dates. Your deposit carries over at no charge.

What you own

The code, repositories, cloud accounts, API keys, data and documentation are yours from the start. If we stop working together, you keep everything and have nothing to buy back. The only things that remain mine are general-purpose tools and code components I reuse across projects, never your data and never your brand.

Data, confidentiality and AI

  • I access only what the work needs, and at handover you decide whether my access stays for monthly care or is removed;
  • your data stays in your accounts, and I keep no copies after the engagement ends;
  • a model receives only the data a task needs, from providers you approve;
  • what I learn about your business stays confidential.

Your side

One decision-maker, access to the systems and the people involved, and answers within a reasonable time. That is what keeps a two-to-three-week build on schedule.
