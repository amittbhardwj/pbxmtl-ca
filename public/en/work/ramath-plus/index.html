Ramath+ — bilingual tutoring platform

Designed and built by Amitt Bhardwj · in service

Ramath+ is my first real project, and the reason I build websites for other businesses. It is not a brochure site: it is a bilingual tutoring platform with verified accounts, parent and tutor portals, session booking and a private online classroom.

What it contains

  • a complete French and English interface, served from a single code base;
  • verified accounts for parents and the tutor;
  • session booking with a calendar;
  • a private online classroom for lessons;
  • student progress tracking.

What I am building right now

I build this platform in my own hours, alongside a full-time job. What I deliver in five months of evenings and weekends is what a $799 launch site uses as its foundation: a clear structure, content in both languages, and a layout that works on a phone.

In other words: I can build bigger than what I sell. Custom projects, portals and automation exist in my practice, but they are not part of the public offer.

What it means for your project

The $799 CAD launch site applies that same discipline at a much smaller and safer scope: one page, six sections, one clear action. You get the same rigour without paying for a platform.
