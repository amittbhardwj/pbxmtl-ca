Ramath+: a bilingual tutoring platform with AI drafting

Designed, built and maintained by Amitt Bhardwj · live at ramathplus.ca

Ramath+ is a Montréal high-school math tutoring business. Its platform is where parents book sessions, students learn in a private online classroom, and the tutor runs the business from one dashboard, in French or English.

What the platform does

  • parent and tutor accounts with passwordless email sign-in;
  • session booking, invoices and a private online classroom;
  • homework, diagnostics and progress tracking for each student;
  • one code base serving the full French and English interface.

Where the AI is, and where it isn’t

  • Session recaps. The tutor writes a few bullet points and the AI drafts a parent update in French and English. The prompt carries the notes and the topic, never the student’s name or email.
  • Monthly reports. Built from the tutor’s own progress records. Only scores and skill labels reach the model, and the student is referred to as “the student.”
  • Replies to parents. Drafted inside fixed rules: four sentences at most, no promised grades, no price other than the published ones, never a word about another student.
  • Homework hints. Socratic by design. Without an attempt, the student only gets what the question asks and a first step, and the model never gives the final answer.

The tutor edits and sends every message; nothing goes to a family automatically. If the model is unavailable, each feature falls back to a plain template, so the tutor can always finish the job.

Stack

A Next.js-style app on Cloudflare Workers (vinext), with D1 and Drizzle for data, passwordless sign-in through hashed, expiring email links, and Resend for email.

What it shows

AI is most useful when it drafts the messages a business writes every day and a person stays accountable for what is sent. The same pattern fits any team that answers the same kinds of questions over and over.
