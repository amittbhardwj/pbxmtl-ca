Spin and Snap: software for a 360° photobooth business

Designed and built by Amitt Bhardwj · studio app live, rental site in preview

Spin and Snap is my 360° and mirror photobooth rental business in Montréal. Instead of paying for a subscription booth app built around someone else’s workflow, I built the two pieces the business needs: a studio app to run each event, and a rental site that turns a quote request into a booking.

The studio app

  • event setup with reusable templates;
  • video, spin and photo capture in the browser, with slow-motion and speed effects;
  • captures saved on the device first, so a dropped connection doesn’t lose them;
  • galleries and QR-code share pages for guests;
  • automatic clean-up after 60 days, on Cloudflare Workers, D1 and private R2 storage, within the free tier.

Once deployed, it passed a 20-check smoke test against the live service, and a real browser capture went from recording to a shareable page in about 20 seconds.

The rental site

  • a French-first bilingual page, with every price kept in one configuration file;
  • quote requests checked on the server, with Cloudflare Turnstile against bots;
  • requests stored in Supabase behind row-level security, so the public can submit but never read;
  • a priced quote by email within minutes, with a link to request the date;
  • every booking confirmed or declined by the operator, never by the software alone.

AI is optional and minimal: it can add one internal checklist tag from the event type, booth and hours, and plain rules do the same job when it is off. Names, contact details, dates, prices and booking decisions never reach the model. The rental site is built and tested end to end on a local test harness, and is not yet open to the public.

Stack

Cloudflare Workers, D1 and R2 for the studio; static HTML and Tailwind, Supabase Postgres and Edge Functions, Turnstile and Resend for the rental site.

What it shows

Automation a small business can afford to run: low hosting costs, prices in one place, a person confirming every booking, and AI only where it cannot leak anything.
