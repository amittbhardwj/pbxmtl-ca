Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before we start.

What exactly is included for $799?+

One bilingual French-and-English page with up to six sections, a contact or booking integration, basic SEO and mobile setup, domain launch support and one revision round.

Why is the price lower than a typical custom website?+

This is a temporary founding-client pilot with a repeatable structure, client-supplied approved content and a tightly controlled scope. It is not a custom application or unlimited design engagement.

Why only one project per month?+

PBX MTL is a deliberately limited independent practice. The capacity limit protects delivery quality and prevents promises that cannot be kept.

Who provides the French and English content?+

You provide the final approved copy, logo and images. I organize and polish the presentation, but professional copywriting and translation are outside the pilot price.

How does payment work?+

A 50% deposit reserves the agreed project month. The remaining 50% is due after the revision round and before the site launches.

What happens after launch?+

Care Lite is optional at $49 per month for light technical care. Custom AI, e-commerce, portals and automations require a separate future scope and are not part of this pilot.
