Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before we start.

What does forward-deployed mean?+

I work inside your business instead of from a spec. I spend time with the people doing the work, ship a first version on real data early, and stay until it runs reliably. It is how large AI and data companies put their software to work at customer sites; I offer the same model to smaller teams.

What kind of AI work do you take on?+

Work that is frequent, text-heavy and easy to check: turning inquiries into quotes, drafting replies and reports for review, summarizing and routing requests, answering questions from internal documents, and adding a careful AI feature to an existing product. I don’t build systems that make financial, medical, legal or hiring decisions on their own.

How much does it cost?+

An audit starts at $750 CAD and takes about a week; the fee is credited if you go ahead with a build. A build starts at $4,500 CAD for two to three weeks. Ongoing care starts at $300 CAD a month. Every price is fixed in writing before work starts, plus applicable taxes.

Who owns the code and the data?+

You do. The code lives in your repository, the services run in your cloud accounts, and the API keys are issued to you. If we stop working together, nothing breaks and nothing has to be moved away from me.

Which AI models do you use?+

Whichever fits the task and your rules: OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or open-weight models. I use the cheapest model that does the job reliably, and every AI step has a fallback that keeps working if a provider goes down.

What happens to our private data?+

It stays in your accounts. A model only receives what the task needs: Ramath+, for example, sends scores and topic labels, never a student’s name or email. When a step needs personal data, we agree on it and write it down.

Do you work in French?+

I work in English. The products I ship can be fully bilingual: Ramath+ and Spin and Snap both run in French and English.

Can you work on-site?+

Yes, anywhere in Greater Montréal. Elsewhere in Canada I work remotely, with a visit when the project needs one.

Are you open to a full-time role?+

Yes. I’m looking for forward-deployed, AI engineering and senior Python roles in Montréal or remote in Canada. Choose Hiring in the contact form, or email me for my CV.

How soon can you start?+

I take one build at a time, so the start date depends on what is in progress. You get a real date on our first call.
